How to Buy Used Industrial Machinery in Pakistan

Buying a used industrial machine is one of the highest-value purchases a Pakistani workshop or factory makes. Done right, it gives you production-grade capability at 30–60% of new-machine cost. Done wrong, you pay for a machine that sits idle, costs more to fix than it did to buy, or never arrives as described. This guide covers what to check, what prices are realistic, how to pay safely, and how to get the machine to your door — based on 11+ years of selling used industrial equipment from Sialkot.

Why buy used industrial machinery in Pakistan

The economic case is straightforward: a new CNC wire cut EDM costs PKR 15–40 lakh. A used unit in working condition costs PKR 5–12 lakh. A new rotary surface grinder costs PKR 12–25 lakh new; a proven used one costs PKR 4–6 lakh. The savings — typically 40–70% of new-machine price — fund tooling, jigs, fixturing, consumables, and first-year operating costs.

Beyond price, Pakistan's industrial ecosystem makes used machines a practical choice. Spare parts for common European, Japanese, and Chinese machines are available in Sialkot, Lahore's Hall Road, Karachi's Saddar market, and Gujranwala's electrical bazaar. Local technicians familiar with most machine types are accessible. And unlike a new import, a used machine in Sialkot is physically present — you can inspect it, run it, and take delivery the same week.

What to inspect before buying

Never buy a used industrial machine from photos alone. Request a live WhatsApp video call with the machine running under power. For any machine, a responsible seller will demonstrate: the machine switched on, all axes moving or all heads spinning, a sample operation on a real workpiece, and any known defects disclosed up front. If the seller refuses a live video, treat that as a significant red flag.

For specific machine types: on a CNC wire cut EDM, ask for a test cut on a steel block — the result should be a clean, burr-free edge with no wire breaks. On a rotary surface grinder, check that the magnetic chuck magnetizes evenly and the spindle has no runout vibration. On a drill press or milling machine, run the spindle at each speed setting and listen for bearing noise. On a rectifier, request an output test under load — the ammeter should hold steady at the rated current.

An on-site visit before purchase is always the safest option. If you are buying from another city, factor in the travel cost — it is almost always worth it for a PKR 2 lakh+ purchase.

Understanding prices in Pakistan

Pakistani used machinery prices are negotiated, not fixed. The asking price is typically 10–20% above the seller's walk-away price, so a respectful counter-offer in that range is normal and expected. Prices vary significantly by machine age, hours of use, brand (Japanese and European machines command 20–40% premiums over Chinese-origin equivalents), and how urgently the seller needs to move the machine.

Rough PKR benchmarks for common used machines in working condition (2025–2026): wire cut EDM CNC PKR 4–12 lakh; rotary surface grinder PKR 3–8 lakh; vibratory finishing machine (large bowl) PKR 3–8 lakh; 5-head drill press PKR 75,000–3 lakh; DC rectifier 1000 A PKR 2–6 lakh; tool and cutter grinder PKR 2–5 lakh; vertical milling machine PKR 2–6 lakh. Prices for CNC-equipped machines trend higher; manual-only versions are cheaper. Machines sourced directly from a working factory are generally in better condition than those that have been idle in storage.

Payment and paperwork in Pakistan

The safest payment method for used industrial machinery in Pakistan is IBFT (Interbank Funds Transfer) — transfer directly to a bank account in the seller's business or personal name. This creates a paper trail and confirms the seller's real identity. Cash in hand works for smaller purchases (under PKR 1 lakh) where you can meet the seller in person. A crossed cheque is suitable for larger transactions between parties who have met and verified each other.

A 20–30% advance is the standard industry norm to hold a machine while you arrange transport, finance, or inspection. The balance is paid on delivery or just before loading. If a seller demands 100% payment in advance before you have seen the machine in person or on live video, consider an escrow arrangement or insist on an in-person visit first. For business buyers who need to capitalize the asset on a balance sheet or claim input tax, request an invoice against the seller's NTN (National Tax Number) — most established dealers can provide one.

Transport and delivery across Pakistan

Within Punjab — Lahore, Gujranwala, Gujrat, Jhelum, Sheikhupura, Wazirabad, Daska, Sambrial, Hafizabad, Mandi Bahauddin, Narowal, Pasrur, Kharian — road freight from Sialkot costs approximately PKR 5,000–15,000 for a mid-size machine (500–2,000 kg), depending on distance and vehicle size. Most Sialkot-based dealers handle loading onto the transport vehicle at their end; unloading at the buyer's factory is the buyer's responsibility.

For buyers outside Punjab — Peshawar, Hayatabad Industrial Estate, Faisalabad, Rawalpindi, Karachi — cargo services such as Daewoo Express Cargo and TCS Cargo handle industrial equipment across Pakistan. Freight costs for a 1,000 kg machine from Sialkot to Karachi run approximately PKR 20,000–40,000. For heavy machinery (3,000 kg+), a dedicated flatbed truck is more practical. Transit time is typically 2–4 days to Karachi, 1–2 days to KPK cities.

Installation support — leveling, electrical connection, and first-run commissioning — is not always included in the purchase price. Ask the seller whether a technician is available (at cost or free) to attend the first run at your factory. For CNC machines, a calibration test cut after transport is essential before beginning production.

Red flags to avoid

Buying used machinery in Pakistan carries real risks if you do not know what to watch for. Treat the following as immediate warnings: a seller who refuses a live video with the machine running; advance payment demanded in full before any in-person or video inspection; a price significantly below the market range (a PKR 5 lakh machine offered for PKR 1.5 lakh is almost certainly either stolen or non-functional); a seller whose bank account is in a different name than the business they describe; and a machine described as being in one city but available for pickup from another location the seller controls.

OLX and other classified platforms are high-risk for high-value machinery purchases because they offer no buyer protection and no identity verification of sellers. Buying from an established dealer at a known physical address — with a contactable phone number, verifiable bank account, and Google Maps location — reduces your risk significantly.

Frequently asked questions

What is a fair price for used industrial machinery in Pakistan?

Used industrial machinery in Pakistan typically sells for 30–60% of the new-machine price. A used CNC wire cut EDM that costs PKR 15–40 lakh new sells used for PKR 5–12 lakh depending on condition. Always compare at least two options and request a live video before agreeing to a price.

How do I pay safely for a used machine in Pakistan?

IBFT bank transfer to the seller's verified account is the safest method. A 20–30% advance is normal to hold the machine; pay the balance on delivery or at loading. Never pay 100% in advance to someone you haven't verified in person or on video.

How do I transport a machine from Sialkot to Lahore or Karachi?

Within Punjab (120 km from Sialkot), road freight is PKR 5,000–15,000 for a mid-size machine. To Karachi or KPK, Daewoo Express Cargo and TCS Cargo handle industrial equipment at PKR 15,000–40,000 depending on weight. The seller handles loading at their end.

Can I get an official invoice with NTN for a used machine purchase?

Yes — established dealers in Sialkot can issue an invoice against their NTN for business buyers who need to book the asset or claim input tax. Ask before agreeing to purchase if this is a requirement for your business.
