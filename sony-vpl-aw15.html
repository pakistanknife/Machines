About this Sony VPL-AW15

The Sony VPL-AW15 is a home theater projector using Sony's SXRD (Silicon X-tal Reflective Display) technology — a variant of LCOS (Liquid Crystal on Silicon) that delivers deep blacks, high contrast, and smooth motion without the rainbow effect common in DLP projectors. It was positioned as an entry-level home cinema machine when new, and still performs well for movie nights, presentations, and conference rooms.

With 1300 ANSI lumens and a 15,000:1 dynamic contrast ratio, it handles both bright scenes and dark scenes convincingly. The 720p native panel (1280×720) is sharp enough for standard HD content — films, sports, YouTube, TV channels — projected at any size from 40 to 200 inches diagonal.
