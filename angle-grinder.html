About this Adjustable Angle Grinder

An adjustable-angle industrial grinder is a multi-purpose grinding machine where the tilting work head and adjustable angle settings allow the operator to sharpen a wide range of cutting tools — drill bits, milling cutters, lathe tool bits, planer tools, taps, and reamers — without needing a dedicated machine for each. It is a classic mainstay of any tool & die shop and a productivity multiplier for small-batch tool maintenance.

The "adjustable" aspect refers to the swivel and tilt of the grinding head and the work table, which combine to set the rake, clearance, and helix angles needed for each cutter geometry. With proper jigs and a good ceramic, CBN or diamond wheel, this machine keeps an entire tool shop’s cutters sharp — and saves the cost of throw-away tooling that adds up quickly in any production environment.
