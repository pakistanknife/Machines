DSP controller — why no PC is needed

The DSP (Digital Signal Processor) handheld controller is the most important feature for most buyers in Pakistan. Unlike Mach3 or LinuxCNC setups that require a dedicated PC running during the entire job, the DSP controller operates fully standalone:

  1. Design your part on any PC using ArtCAM, UcanCAM, Type3, Fusion 360, or any CAD/CAM software that outputs standard G-code.
  2. Transfer the G-code file to a USB drive.
  3. Plug the USB into the DSP handset, select the file, set your work zero, and press Start.
  4. The machine runs — the PC can be switched off, used for other work, or taken elsewhere.

This means one PC serves the design work for multiple machines, and the production floor does not need desktop computers at each machine. The handset also gives the operator direct jog control, feed-rate override, and emergency stop — all within reach without touching a keyboard.

Running a CNC router in Pakistan — what you need to know

Power: The machine runs on standard 220V single-phase supply — the same supply used by an air conditioner or a large lathe. At full spindle load, it draws approximately 3–4 kW. In areas of Punjab and Sindh with voltage instability, a 5–7 kVA servo voltage stabilizer (PKR 15,000–30,000 from local suppliers) protects the spindle inverter and stepper drivers from fluctuation damage. Without stabilization, voltage spikes are the single biggest cause of inverter failure on Chinese CNC machines in Pakistan.

Tooling: End mills for this machine — 4mm, 6mm, and 8mm diameter carbide bits for wood and acrylic, ball-nose for 3D relief work, V-bits for engraving — are available from CNC tooling suppliers in Lahore's Anarkali and Hall Road markets, Karachi's Electronics Market, and several online suppliers who ship across Pakistan. Budget PKR 800–3,000 per bit depending on coating and size.

Software: ArtCAM is the most widely used CNC design software in Pakistan's CNC workshops — tutorials and crack copies are widely shared, though a legitimate licence is recommended for production work. UcanCAM and Type3 are alternatives. All produce DSP-compatible G-code. For knife handles, many operators use simple Fusion 360 free edition and a basic post-processor.

Maintenance: Water-cooled spindles require monthly checks on the coolant water quality (use distilled water and a small amount of antifreeze). The linear rails and ballscrews need lubrication every 50–100 running hours with white lithium grease — available from any hardware shop in Sialkot or Lahore.
