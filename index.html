Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying — feel free to ask anything else on WhatsApp.

Are these machines used or new?

All machines on this page are used (second-hand) industrial machines. Each unit has been inspected and is in working condition. We sell directly from our facility in Sialkot — no broker, no markup.

Can I see the machines before buying?

Yes. We are located at Shahpura Road Industrial Area, Sialkot, Pakistan, and welcome on-site visits by appointment. For buyers who cannot visit, we provide live video walk-throughs and demonstrations on WhatsApp before any commitment.

Do you deliver to Lahore, Gujranwala or other Punjab cities?

Yes. We deliver across Punjab to all major cities within ~120 km of Sialkot — including Lahore, Gujranwala, Gujrat, Jhelum, Sheikhupura, Wazirabad, Daska, Sambrial, Hafizabad, Mandi Bahauddin, Narowal, Pasrur and Kharian. Local pickup is also welcome at our facility on Shahpura Road. Transport cost is added based on distance and machine size — WhatsApp for exact quote.

What payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfer, cash on collection, or local mobile wallets (Easypaisa, JazzCash, Raast). Typical practice: small deposit on confirmation, balance on delivery or collection. Details on WhatsApp.

What is included with each machine?

Standard tooling and accessories where available, original manuals where preserved, and a maintenance log. Specialty tooling can be supplied on request — please specify your needs and we will quote.

Why buy used industrial machines from Sialkot, Pakistan?

Sialkot is the world’s leading hub for surgical instruments and a major center for cutlery, sports goods, and precision tooling. The local industry has decades of experience with these machines, which means easy access to spare parts, consumables, and service. Buying directly from a Sialkot owner means no broker, fair pricing, and machines that have already proven themselves in serious production.
