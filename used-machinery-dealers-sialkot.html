Sialkot as a used machinery market

Sialkot is Pakistan's primary industrial export city — surgical instruments, cutlery, sports goods, and leather goods are manufactured here for European and North American markets. That production base means the city has a deep supply of industrial machinery: toolroom grinders, wire cut EDM machines, drill presses, plating rectifiers, and vibratory finishing machines that have been used in working export factories and are now available for resale.

The Shahpura Road Industrial Area and the surrounding estates — Wazirabad Road, Sambrial, Daska — are where most of Sialkot's used machinery changes hands. Buyers from Lahore, Gujranwala, Gujrat, Narowal, and Pasrur regularly make the drive to Sialkot for toolroom equipment that is difficult to find at comparable prices in other Punjab cities. For factories upgrading to newer CNC equipment, the outgoing machines are often in excellent working condition.

Unlike machinery markets in Karachi or Lahore, Sialkot's supply is closely tied to the export industry cycle — machines that appear on the market have typically run in ISO-regulated export factories where maintenance was taken seriously. This is a meaningful difference for buyers who want a used machine that will hold tolerances in production.

Industries we serve

Our customers in Sialkot and across Punjab include:

  • Surgical instruments manufacturers — wire cut EDM for die-making, surface grinders for blade grinding, drill presses for precision hole-making in forceps and clamps.
  • Cutlery factories — stamping die EDM, vibratory finishers for deburring spoons and forks, plating rectifiers for electropolishing stainless steel.
  • Automotive and general engineering — surface grinders for fixture plates, milling machines for component machining, rectifiers for anodizing or electroplating small parts.
  • Leather goods and sports goods factories — drill presses for riveting dies, angle grinders for tooling prep and weld cleanup.
  • General toolrooms across Punjab (Gujranwala, Gujrat, Wazirabad, Lahore) buying used equipment to start or expand a job-shop.
