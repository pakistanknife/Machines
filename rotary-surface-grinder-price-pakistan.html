Rotary vs reciprocating surface grinder — which to buy?

Rotary — best for

Round parts, small flat workpieces, high-volume batch grinding (surgical blanks, washers, stampings). Very high throughput — 300–800 pieces per hour on small parts. Circular magnetic chuck is easy to load quickly.

Reciprocating — best for

Long flat workpieces, blocks, molds, and flat dies that don't fit a circular chuck. Better for single large parts or profiles. Standard in tool rooms and mold shops across Lahore and Gujranwala.

What affects the price of a rotary surface grinder?

1. Magnetic chuck diameter and flatness

Larger chucks cost more and accommodate bigger parts. More importantly, chuck flatness determines grinding accuracy — a worn or demagnetised chuck is expensive to recondition (PKR 15,000–40,000). Always test the chuck before purchase.

2. Spindle bearing condition

The spindle is the heart of the machine. Worn bearings cause vibration, poor surface finish, and tool chatter. A spindle rebuild on a rotary surface grinder costs PKR 20,000–60,000. Check for runout with a dial gauge — acceptable runout is under 0.002 mm; above 0.005 mm indicates worn bearings.

3. Table speed control

Variable-speed chucks (electronically controlled) allow matching rotation speed to part size and material — important for consistent Ra finish on surgical blanks. Fixed-speed machines are cheaper but less versatile.

4. Wheel dresser quality

A precision diamond dresser in good condition is worth PKR 8,000–20,000 on its own. Machines sold with quality dressing attachments are ready to produce on day one.

What is the difference between a rotary and a reciprocating surface grinder? +
A rotary surface grinder has a circular rotating magnetic chuck — it grinds round parts and small flat components in a continuous rotation, achieving 300–800 pcs/hr on small parts. A reciprocating surface grinder has a rectangular table that moves back and forth under the wheel — better for long flat workpieces, blocks, and molds. For Sialkot's high-volume small-parts production (surgical blanks, washers, stampings), rotary grinders deliver significantly higher throughput.
